# Option 1 · Modern Pixar — dark-studio glow-up

Friendly stylized 3D, but art-directed to the app's dark premium palette.
The characters stay warm and likeable; the presentation stops being cutesy.

## Style DNA (prepend to every identity line)

> Rendered 3D Pixar-style character head, soft matte skin with subtle
> subsurface scattering, big expressive eyes with soft catch-lights, sculpted
> stylized hair, confident relaxed expression. Studio portrait on a deep
> navy-black background (#0B1220) with a soft cool-blue rim light from the
> right and a warm gentle key light from the upper-left, faint blue ambient
> glow behind the head. Head and shoulders, centered, facing forward.
> Square, 1024x1024. No text.

## Negative prompt

```
text, watermark, photorealistic photo, uncanny, harsh shadows, flat 2D vector,
line-art, full body, hands, multiple people, busy background, sparkles, hearts,
warm amber background, orange glow, low resolution
```
