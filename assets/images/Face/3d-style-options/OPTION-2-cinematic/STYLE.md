# Option 2 · Cinematic Semi-Realistic — premium game-character render

Grown-up, moody character portraits like a modern AAA character-select
screen. Least cartoony of the three; highest perceived production value.

## Style DNA (prepend to every identity line)

> High-end stylized-realistic 3D game character portrait, detailed skin
> texture with pores softened, realistic eyes, physically-based hair strands,
> subtle film grain. Dramatic dark studio: deep navy-black background
> (#0B1220), strong cool-blue rim light tracing one side of the face, soft
> warm key light, shallow depth of field. Neutral confident expression with a
> hint of a smile. Head and shoulders, centered, facing camera. Square,
> 1024x1024. No text.

## Negative prompt

```
text, watermark, cartoon, chibi, anime, flat vector, uncanny dead eyes,
overexposed, harsh flash, full body, hands, multiple people, busy background,
bright background, low resolution
```
