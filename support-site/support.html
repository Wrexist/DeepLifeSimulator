How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-app Store or Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Eligible permanent unlocks and active subscriptions can be restored. Consumable gems, money and spent revives are not refilled by Restore Purchases. For a pack that includes both unlocks and currency, restoration does not re-grant the currency.

I paid, but the reward is missing or awaiting recovery.

Keep the app installed. Load the original character and save slot used for the purchase, then tap "Restore Purchases". If the purchase remains pending, contact support before buying again. Include the product name, approximate purchase time, app version, device model and the exact message shown. You can include a receipt with unrelated details removed. Never send your password, verification codes or full payment-card details. Recovery depends on the transaction and the saved purchase record, so we cannot promise that reinstalling will recover a missing consumable reward.

The store won't load — what do I do?

Make sure you're online and updated to the latest version. The store now retries gracefully if the catalog is slow to load. If it persists, force-close and reopen the app, then email us your device model and iOS version.

Is my progress saved? Can I lose it?

Your progress is saved locally on your device. If a save error appears, keep the app installed and contact support before resetting or deleting data. Deleting the app removes local saves. Restore Purchases restores eligible purchases, not your character, save slots or consumable balance. Do not rely on reinstalling or changing devices to recover a life.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

We'd love to hear it. Email DeepLifeSimulator@gmail.com with a short description and a screenshot if you can. Player feedback directly shapes what we build next.

Is DeepLife available on Android?

DeepLife Simulator is live on iOS today, with Android in development. Keep an eye on this site for the announcement.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong on our end, email us and we'll help sort it out.

The game is running slowly or crashed.

Update to the latest version, restart your device, and make sure you have free storage. If a crash keeps happening, email us the exact step that triggers it plus your device model and iOS version so we can reproduce and fix it.
