How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-app Store and tap "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. The latest update fixes the "products not configured" error, so the catalog should load reliably. Still stuck? Email us.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your life is saved automatically on your device after every meaningful action. Season rewards now auto-collect across rollovers so nothing is lost.

The store won't load — what do I do?

Make sure you're online and on the newest version (2.5.1). The store now retries gracefully if the catalog is slow. If it persists, restart the app or email us your device model and iOS version.

Is DeepLife on Android?

DeepLife Simulator is live on iOS today, with Android in the works. Watch this page for the announcement.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email DeepLifeSimulator@gmail.com with a short description (and a screenshot if you can). Player feedback directly shapes what we build next.
