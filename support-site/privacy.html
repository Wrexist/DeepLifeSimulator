Privacy policy

Plain and simple: your game lives on your device, we don't sell your data, and you stay in control.

App: DeepLife Sim Last updated: October 20, 2025 Version: 1.0.2

★ Quick summary

  • What we collect: game data (stored locally), device info, and optional tracking data (only with your permission).
  • How we use it: to save your progress, improve the app, and show ads only if you allow it.
  • Third parties: Apple (in-app purchases), Expo (deployment), AdMob (ads — currently disabled).
  • Your control: you own your data. Delete the app and your data goes with it. Opt out anytime.
  • Age: rated 17+, not intended for children under 13.

1 Information we collect

  • Game Data: your progress, statistics, achievements, save slots, and in-game preferences, stored locally on your device.
  • Device Information: device type, OS version, app version, and device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: how you interact with the app, features used, session duration, and gameplay patterns.
  • Purchase Data: in-app purchase receipts and history, processed securely through Apple.
  • Tracking Data (optional): with your explicit permission via App Tracking Transparency, we may collect data for personalized advertising.

2 How we use your information

  • App functionality: save your progress locally, provide game features, and process purchases securely.
  • User experience: improve performance, fix bugs, and enhance gameplay.
  • Advertising (with permission): show relevant ads through Google AdMob.
  • Customer support: respond to your questions, feedback, and technical issues.
  • Legal compliance: comply with applicable laws and protect our legal rights.

3 Third-party services

  • Apple App Store: secure in-app purchase processing and app distribution.
  • Expo: app development, deployment, and over-the-air updates.
  • Google AdMob (currently disabled): displaying ads, requires your consent via ATT when enabled.

We do not use Firebase or other analytics beyond Apple's built-in frameworks, and we do not transmit your game data to external servers unless you explicitly enable cloud save. All progress is stored locally using AsyncStorage.

4 Data storage & transmission

  • Local storage: all game data is stored on your device.
  • No cloud by default: your data stays on your device unless you enable cloud save.
  • In-app purchases: receipts are validated with Apple but not stored on our servers.
  • Data deletion: deleting the app permanently removes all locally stored game data.

5 Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Apple Inc.: in-app purchase processing and app distribution.
  • Expo: deployment and crash reporting (minimal device/app info only).
  • Google AdMob (when enabled): serving ads if you grant tracking permission.
  • Legal authorities: only when required by law or to protect legal rights.

6 App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

  • We request your permission before tracking your activity across other apps and websites.
  • You can grant or deny this when first launching the app.
  • Denying tracking does not affect core app functionality.
  • Change your preference anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
  • Tracking is only used for personalized advertising when AdMob is enabled.

7 Data security

  • Encryption: sensitive data is encrypted using industry-standard methods.
  • Secure transmission: all network communication uses HTTPS.
  • Local security: game data relies on iOS device security features.
  • No passwords: the app requires no account or password storage.
  • Purchase security: all transactions go through Apple's secure payment system.

8 Data retention

  • Game data: stored locally until you delete the app or your save files.
  • Purchase receipts: retained by Apple for 7 years (tax and legal compliance).
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days for debugging.
  • Analytics data: not collected in the current version.

9 Your rights

  • Access: view all your game data in-app (Statistics, Progress screens).
  • Correct: change your character name and preferences in Settings.
  • Delete: remove all data by deleting the app or using in-app save-slot deletion.
  • Opt out of tracking: deny or revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • Opt out of ads: purchase "Remove Ads" when available.
  • Portability: export your game data (cloud save feature, when enabled).
  • Contact us: email DeepLifeSimulator@gmail.com with any privacy concerns.

10 Children's privacy (COPPA)

  • The app is rated 17+ and is not intended for children under 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If you believe we have, contact us immediately at DeepLifeSimulator@gmail.com.
  • Parents can use iOS parental controls to restrict usage and in-app purchases.

11 In-app purchases

  • All purchases are processed securely through Apple's In-App Purchase system.
  • We do not store your payment information — we only receive transaction receipts (no card data).
  • Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored via "Restore Purchases".
  • Refunds are handled by Apple according to their refund policy.

12 Changes to this policy

  • We may update this policy from time to time.
  • We'll notify you of significant changes by updating the app and posting the new policy.
  • The "Last updated" date above indicates the most recent revision.
  • Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

13 International users

  • The app is available worldwide through the Apple App Store.
  • By using it, you consent to data transfer to the United States, where our service providers may be located.
  • We comply with applicable international privacy laws including GDPR and CCPA.

14 Contact us

Questions, concerns, or requests about this policy?

  • Email: DeepLifeSimulator@gmail.com
  • App: DeepLife Sim
  • Developer: DeepLife Simulator
  • Response time: we aim to reply within 48 hours.

15 Compliance

This privacy policy complies with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — EU
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — US
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) — US
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — iOS 14.5+
