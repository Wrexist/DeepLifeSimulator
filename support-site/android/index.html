What is Deep Life Simulator?

A life, simulated properly.

Not a tap-to-win idle game. A life with a budget, a calendar and consequences.

🗓️

ONE CHOICE A WEEK

Every week you pick one thing. Work, study, rest, risk it. The week ticks, the bills land, and the world moves whether you were ready or not.

🧮

THE MONEY IS REAL

Rent, income tax and student loan repayments come out weekly. A bill you cannot pay becomes arrears you carry — the money axis has a failure state.

🪜

35 CAREER TRACKS

From fast food at $110 a week to surgeon, CEO or President. Promotions gate on performance, not on time served.

🧬

YOU DIE. THE STORY DOESN'T.

Marry, raise children, pass on traits and money. When your life ends you play your heir, and their grandchildren after that.

🎬

23 WAYS TO START

Rags to Riches, Debt Escape, Criminal Empire, Political Dynasty, Late Bloomer, Lottery Winner — pick the life you begin with.

Eight systems, one life

Each one has its own economy. All of them bill the same wallet.

What we actually want back

Ratings are nice. These are better:

  • The moment you got confused. The first time you didn't know what to do next — that's a design bug, and we can't see it from in here.

  • The moment you got bored. "I stopped playing at week 40" is the single most useful sentence you can send.

  • Anything that crashed, froze or lost your save. There's a bug form with your device details already filled in.
