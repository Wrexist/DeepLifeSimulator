What's new.

A fairer, more reliable DeepLife with every release. Here's what changed.

Version 2.5.1 LATEST
  • 🛒
    Purchases fixed. Resolved the "products not configured" error so the in-app store loads reliably, and rebuilt the purchase system on a modern foundation for more dependable checkout and restores.
  • ⚖️
    Economy rebalanced. Closed a wide batch of money exploits and loopholes (pet competitions, study spam, jail payouts and more) so your wealth reflects real progress and the late game stays challenging.
  • 🎁
    Season rewards fixed. Unclaimed rewards are now automatically collected when a season rolls over, so you'll never lose what you earned.
  • ✨
    Stability & polish. A range of behind-the-scenes fixes for a smoother, more reliable experience.
Earlier — v1.8.7 Stability & Balance
  • 💰
    Financial overhaul. Reworked bankruptcy, debt collection, loans and lifestyle costs for a more believable money game.
  • 🏥
    Health & happiness. Overhauled the disease system and tuned stat decay and recovery.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Family & poverty. Balanced child expenses and added a clearer path out of poverty.
  • ⚖️
    Crime & justice. Improved wanted levels and the jail system.

Always update to the latest version for the best, most balanced experience.
