Descriptions

One line

A free life sim for iPhone and Android where you live one week at a time, with a real economy and an heir who inherits it all.

Short (about 50 words)

Deep Life Simulator is a free, text-first life simulation game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Each tap is one week: you get paid, rent and bills come out, and the stock market moves. Build a career, invest, buy property, start a company or a family, and pass your fortune to your heir.

Long (about 120 words)

Deep Life Simulator starts you as a young adult with next to nothing and asks how you will spend each week. Work, study, hustle, invest, date, rest or take a risk: every choice moves your money, health, energy, happiness and relationships, and the effects compound over decades.

The money is real inside the game. Salaries come weekly, bills that go unpaid become arrears, loans charge interest, and a stock market and crypto market move every week. You can buy and rent out property, found a company and take it public, or run for office.

When your character dies, you get an obituary of the life you lived, and your heir takes over the fortune, the family name and whatever mess you left behind.

What makes it different

  • Week by week. Time moves one week per tap, so rent, food and a missed bill all matter, not just the big decisions.
  • An economy that pushes back. Interest, arrears, a moving market, property values and companies with an IPO.
  • Generations. Heirs inherit wealth and a Mindset; legacy points and dynasty tiers carry across lives.
  • Fair by design. No real-time energy timer, full-screen ads only at year-end breaks, and the whole game is free.
