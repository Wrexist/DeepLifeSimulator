# Apple featuring nominations

Free, about 15 minutes each, and the main way an indie game gets in front of the
App Store editors. Nominate **every** content update, not just big ones.

**Where:** App Store Connect › Apps › Deep Life Simulator › **Featuring** ›
Nominations › Create. You need the Account Holder, Admin, App Manager or
Marketing role.

**When:** at least 2 weeks before the update goes live. Up to 3 months ahead
for the bigger ones. You can edit a nomination until it is reviewed.

**What Apple reads:** what is new *for players*, why it is interesting now, and
the date. Short and concrete beats long and excited. Mention anything that fits
an editorial theme (Halloween, holidays, new year, a cultural moment).

---

## Nomination 1: the next update (image life cards + holiday events)

- **Type:** App Enhancement
- **Name:** Deep Life Simulator: share the life you lived
- **Expected publish date:** set to your release date (at least 14 days out)
- **Description (paste):**

> Deep Life Simulator is a text-first life sim where every week counts: a job,
> rent, loans with real interest, a stock market that moves, and a family that
> inherits whatever you leave behind.
>
> This update turns the end of a life into something worth sharing. When a
> character dies, players can now share a picture of the life they lived: the
> name, the age, the net worth, the career and the verdict, ready for Messages,
> Instagram Stories or Discord.
>
> Through the holidays the game runs a calendar of timed events: "Nothing to
> Fear" for Halloween, "Home for the Holidays" in December and "New Year, Same
> Ledger" in January, each a small real-life goal with an in-game reward.
>
> Made by an independent developer and free to play on iPhone and iPad.

- **Regions:** All
- **Add:** the App Store product page link and 2–3 screenshots of the new card

## Nomination 3: a one-year or milestone story (use when true)

If a real milestone lands (a download count, a big rating number, a year since
launch), nominate it as **App Enhancement** with the number and what you
shipped that year. Only use numbers you can show in App Store Connect.

## Google Play

Play has no public nomination form. The equivalent levers are:

1. **Promotional content** in Play Console (see `in-app-events.md`), which has a
   "request featuring" box when you submit.
2. Your Google Play partner contact, if you ever get one through a program or
   event.
3. Google Play's indie programs (Indie Games Festival and Accelerator). Check
   `g.co/play/indiefestival` each winter for the next edition's country list.
