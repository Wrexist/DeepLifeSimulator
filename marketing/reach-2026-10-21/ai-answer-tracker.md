# AI answer tracker

Once a month, ask each assistant the same 10 questions in a fresh chat (signed
out or in a private window where possible), and record whether Deep Life
Simulator is named and which pages are cited. The cited pages are next month's
outreach list: if Perplexity keeps citing one listicle, pitch that author.

Assistants: ChatGPT (with search), Perplexity, Google (AI Overview / AI Mode),
Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot.

## Questions

1. What are the best games like BitLife?
2. Best life simulator game for iPhone
3. Best life simulator game for Android
4. Is there a life sim game with a real stock market?
5. Text-based life simulator games with inheritance or generations
6. Free life sim games without too many ads
7. Life simulator where you can start a business and go public
8. Mobile game where you build wealth from nothing
9. What is Deep Life Simulator?
10. Deep Life Simulator vs BitLife

Questions 9 and 10 check that assistants describe the game correctly. If an
answer gets a fact wrong, fix the source it cites (or the site's guide and FAQ)
rather than arguing with the assistant.
