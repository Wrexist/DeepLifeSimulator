# "Games like BitLife" lists and press

"Best of" lists are the page type AI assistants cite most, and they are what
ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google repeat when someone asks "what's like BitLife?".
Getting onto five of these is worth more than anything on our own site.

## Rules for every pitch

- One short email per page. No attachments; links only.
- Lead with what is different, in one line. Do not attack BitLife.
- Always include: store links, the press kit, one 30-second clip, a promo code
  for DeepLife+.
- Follow up once after 7 days. Then stop.
- Log every pitch in the table at the bottom so nobody gets emailed twice.

Links to paste:
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/deep-life-simulator/id6749675615
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.deeplife.simulator
- Site and guide: https://wrexist.github.io/DeepLifeSimulator/guide.html
- Press kit: upload `marketing/press-kit.html` to the support site first, or
  attach as a link to the GitHub Pages copy once deployed.

## Email: press (news hook required)

Only pitch press when there is news: an update, a seasonal event, a milestone,
or a story. Two hooks that are true today:

1. **Update hook:** "a life sim where your death becomes a shareable card"
   (once the image card ships).
2. **Story hook:** a solo developer's numbers story, e.g. "one random number
   made every player live the same life; fixing it more than doubled the events
   players see." (From `tasks/event-delivery-2026-09-04.md`: 12 simulated lives
   went from 33 to 78 distinct events.) Good for dev-focused outlets like Game
   Developer, and for Swedish tech press.

**Subject:** {hook in under 9 words}

> Hi {name},
>
> {One sentence with the hook.}
>
> Deep Life Simulator is a free, text-first life sim for iPhone and Android.
> You play one week at a time: job, rent, loans, a stock market that moves,
> family, and an heir who inherits it all.
>
> What is new: {2–3 bullets}
>
> Clip: {link} · Press kit: {link} · App Store / Google Play: {links}
> Promo code for DeepLife+: {code}
>
> Isac, Deep Life Simulator

Where to send:
- **TouchArcade:** the staff email at the bottom of their forum pages, and
  start a thread in their Upcoming/Released forum with the trailer; staff read
  active threads.
- **Pocket Gamer:** news tips address on their contact page; also enter the
  free Pocket Gamer Mobile Games Awards / Big Indie Pitch when open.
- **Droid Gamers, 148Apps, AppAdvice:** contact pages.
- **Swedish:** local paper, Swedish games and tech sites (a local developer
  story is easier to place than a review).
