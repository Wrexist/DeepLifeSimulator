# Short video and slideshows

Complements `marketing/tiktok_scripts.md` (March 2026) and the three finished
videos in `marketing/videos/`. Everything here is recorded from a **real
playthrough**: never show a result the game did not produce.

## How to run it

- **Cadence:** 3 videos a week for 6 weeks before judging the channel. Same
  file to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, uploaded natively, no
  watermark from another app. Longer cut (3–8 min) to YouTube weekly.
- **Hook in the first second**, on screen as text *and* said out loud.
- **Length:** 15–35 seconds. End on the result (the life card is the perfect
  last frame), not on a logo.
- **Caption:** one line + `#lifesim #bitlife #lifesimulator #DeepLifeSim`.
  Link in bio goes to `https://wrexist.github.io/DeepLifeSimulator/get/?src=tiktok`
  (use `src=reels`, `src=shorts` on the others).
- **Judge by installs**, not views: App Store Connect › Sources and Play
  Console › Acquisition, per `src`.
- **YouTube titles** name the game, because YouTube mentions are one of the
  strongest signals for AI answers: "I became a billionaire in Deep Life
  Simulator (from $0)".

## 10 video scripts

Each: **Hook** (0–1s) → **Setup** (1–5s) → **Montage** (5–25s) → **Result** (last 3s).

1. **"I started at 18 with $0 and a loan."** Setup: take the loan. Montage:
   job, first rent, first stock buy, a bad week. Result: net worth at 30.
2. **"What $10,000 in stocks becomes in 20 years."** Same seed of money, three
   runs: all-in one stock, spread across many, savings account. Result: three
   numbers side by side.
3. **"I let my character do only crime."** Dark web and street jobs only.
   Result: the life card's cause of death and net worth.
4. **"My heir inherited a mess."** End one life in debt, play the heir's first
   year climbing out. Result: heir's net worth vs parent's.
5. **"Four generations, one family business."** Found a company, pass it down.
   Result: generation 4 card.
6. **"The cheapest possible life."** Minimum spending, maximum saving. Result:
   age and savings at death.
7. **"Every choice decided by a coin flip."** Viewer-friendly chaos. Result:
   the card.
8. **"Rate my life: 1 to 10."** Show the card first, then rewind through the
   moments. Ask viewers to rate in comments (comments boost reach).
9. **"I said yes to every date."** Spark dating app, every match. Result:
   relationships, happiness, money.
10. **"Politician speedrun."** Career into politics, campaign, office.
    Result: the title on the obituary.

Seasonal: a Halloween "Nothing to Fear" run (until 6 Nov) and a "Home for the
Holidays" run in December, each pointing at the live in-game event.

## 5 TikTok slideshows

**Ready to post:** A and D are rendered as 1080×1920 images in `/mnt/project-files/reach-2026-10-21/slideshows/` (`make.mjs` there re-renders them; edit the text and run it with Playwright). From X threads that tested the format: 5–6 slides, one idea per slide, a hook
on every slide so people swipe, the game mentioned softly around slide 4–5,
same visual style throughout. Post from a separate niche account about money
and life choices, 1–2 a day. Make variations of anything past ~15k views.

**A. "5 money mistakes that ruin a life (I tested them in a life sim)"**
1. 5 money mistakes that ruin a life. I tested every one.

3. #2 Ignoring bills. They don't vanish, they become arrears.
4. #3 Betting it all on one stock. (screenshot of a crash in Deep Life Simulator)
5. #4 Borrowing to look rich. #5 Neglecting friends. Close bonds kept my character happy.

**B. "I lived 3 lives with the same $1,000"** — one slide per strategy, the
cards on slide 5, "which would you pick?" on 6.

**C. "What your net worth at 30 says about you"** — tiers ($0, $10k, $100k,
$1M) with a life card for each, game named on slide 5.

**D. "POV: you're 18 and just moved out"** — rent, food, first paycheck, the
first bad week, the card.

**E. "Rate these obituaries"** — four real life cards, one per slide, ask for
the best and worst.
