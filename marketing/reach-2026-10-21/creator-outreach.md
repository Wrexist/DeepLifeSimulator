# Creator outreach

Small and mid-size creators (about 1k–100k followers) who already post BitLife,
The Sims or "life simulator" content. They reply more often than big channels,
their viewers trust them, and a free DeepLife+ code costs nothing.

## Finding 30 creators in an hour

1. YouTube search, filter **Upload date: This month**: `bitlife challenge`,
   `life simulator`, `bitlife but`, `life sim game mobile`. Open channels with
   1k–100k subscribers that posted in the last 60 days.
2. TikTok search: `#bitlife`, `#bitlifechallenge`, `#lifesim`,
   `#lifesimulator`. The BitLife challenge tag alone has close to a million
   posts, so there are many small creators.
3. Skip anyone whose comments look bought, or who only posts sponsored content.
4. Write their name, link, followers and **one specific video** in the log below.

Contact: YouTube "About" page business email, TikTok/Instagram bio email, or a
DM if there is none.

## Codes

Give each creator their own DeepLife+ promo code (App Store Connect › Offer
Codes / Promo Codes; Play Console › Promo codes) and their own campaign link so
you can see who brings players:

- iOS: `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6749675615?pt={provider id}&ct={creator}&mt=8`
  (your provider id is in App Store Connect › App Analytics › Campaigns)
- Android: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.deeplife.simulator&referrer=utm_source%3D{creator}%26utm_medium%3Dcreator%26utm_campaign%3Doutreach`
- Or one link for both: `https://wrexist.github.io/DeepLifeSimulator/get/?src={creator}`

> I think a "{challenge idea}" run could be fun for your viewers.
>
> Here's a code for DeepLife+ (no ads): {code}
> Trailer: {link}
>
> No strings attached, I'd just love to see you check it out. If you do make
> something, tell me and I'll share it everywhere I can.

Challenge ideas to pick from, one per creator:
- "Billionaire from nothing before 40"
- "I let my heir inherit a debt-ridden family"
- "Every decision decided by the stock market"
- "Criminal to President in one life"
- "Four generations, one family business"
- "Only street work until age 30"

## Follow-up (once, after 5–7 days)

> Hey {name}, just checking the code reached you. If it's not your thing, no
> worries at all!

## When someone posts

- Thank them publicly and repost to Discord and your socials.
- Ask if they want an early build of the next update.
- Note the campaign link's installs after 7 days.

## About key platforms

Keymailer and Woovit are built for PC and console keys and attract fake
"creators". If you try one, cap the codes and check each applicant's channel by
hand before approving.
