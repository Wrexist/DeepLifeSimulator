# App Store Featuring Nomination — Deep Life Simulator

**Where to submit:** App Store Connect → your app → **App Store** tab →
**Featuring** → *Nominate your app*.
**When:** at least **3 weeks** before your requested feature date; target **6–8
weeks** and align the date with the next update's release.

## Form field 3 — Description / what makes it unique

Paste this block:

```
Deep Life Simulator is a life simulation game with a real economic engine. Unlike
random-tap life sims, every financial decision compounds: you take loans and pay
interest, trade a live weekly market, buy real estate and vehicles that depreciate,
and can genuinely go bankrupt. Good decisions beat luck — that's the whole game.

What makes it stand out:

• A real point of view — "economics that actually matter," not randomized outcomes.
• Player-first by design — no forced ads, a permanent ad-removal option, and no
  pay-to-win. A live money-conservation invariant test fails the build if a single
  dollar could appear from or vanish into nowhere.
• Depth for hundreds of hours — 20+ career paths (doctor to hacker), 80+ life
  events, an in-game phone with mini-apps (dating, contacts, pets, politics, live
  statistics), and a prestige system where each life inherits from the last to build
  generational wealth.
• Built for everyone — 39 App Store localizations, reduced-motion support,
  extensive VoiceOver labeling, and WCAG-AA contrast.
• A guaranteed-fairness system so the sim never feels arbitrary: kids, marriage,
  jobs, and weekly events are all guaranteed after fair attempts.

This update is a turnaround: instant week-advance, ~90% faster income math, 70+
fixes, hardened saves, and anti-exploit economy guards. It's the version we want
new players to meet Deep Life Simulator on.
```

## Notes on odds (internal)

Featuring is editor discretion; most nominations don't convert. Two things move the
needle most for this app: **(1) rating trajectory** — nominate on the back of the
stability update and keep the recent-review sentiment climbing, and **(2) timing to a
real moment** — a scheduled release + a live In-App Event. Submit a clean nomination
for *every* meaningful update; consistency compounds.
