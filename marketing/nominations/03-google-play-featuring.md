# Google Play Featuring & Awards Nomination — Deep Life Simulator

Google Play's editorial featuring works differently from Apple's — there's no single
public "nominate" button for most developers. You maximize your odds through three
channels, all covered here.

---

## Channel 1 — Play Console store-listing signals (do this first)

Editors evaluate what the Console shows them. Make these true before pitching:

- [ ] **Custom store listing** current for the update, with a crisp short
      description (see below).
- [ ] **Feature graphic (1024×500)** that states the hook, not just the logo.
- [ ] **Promo video** (YouTube link) — 30s, gameplay-forward.
- [ ] **Store listing experiments** running (A/B) — signals an active, invested dev.
- [ ] **Pre-registration / release scheduled** for the update, if applicable.
- [ ] **Tags & category** correct: Simulation; secondary tags for strategy / tycoon /
      role playing.
- [ ] **Data safety** form complete and accurate.
- [ ] **Recent reviews being answered** — Google weighs this heavily.

**Short description (80 chars max):**
```
Build wealth from nothing. Make tough choices. Watch it all collapse. Again.
```

## Channel 2 — Editorial featuring pitch (email / partner contact)

If you have a Google Play partner manager or an editorial contact, send this. If
not, keep it on file for when you do, and use the Play Console "Contact us" /
business-development routes.

**Subject:** Featuring consideration — Deep Life Simulator (Simulation) — [update] on [DATE]

**Body:**

```
Hi [name],

We're shipping a major update to Deep Life Simulator (Games › Simulation) on [DATE]
and would love it to be considered for editorial featuring.

Why it's a fit for Play's editorial:

• A real point of view. It's a life sim with a real economic engine — compounding
  loans, a live weekly market, real estate, and genuine bankruptcy — where good
  decisions beat luck, not random taps.

• Player-first monetization. No forced ads, a permanent ad-removal option, and no
  pay-to-win. A live money-conservation check fails our build if a single unit of
  currency is ever created incorrectly.

• Global and inclusive. 39 store localizations, reduced-motion support, extensive
  accessibility labeling, and WCAG-AA contrast.

• Depth. 20+ careers, 80+ life events, an in-game phone with mini-apps, and a
  prestige/generational-wealth loop that turns one session into a multi-generation
  strategy.

• A real turnaround. This update is a top-to-bottom stability, fairness, and
  performance pass: instant week-advance, ~90% faster income math, 70+ fixes,
  hardened saves, and anti-exploit economy guards.

Assets: promo video [link], feature graphic [link], screenshots [link], press kit
[link]. Release is scheduled for [DATE], worldwide.

Thanks for considering it,
[you]
```

## Channel 3 — Programs & awards to enter

- **Google Play's "Best of [year]"** — editorially selected; you don't apply, you
  earn it through sustained quality + engagement. Everything above feeds it.
- **Google Play Indie Games programs** (Festival / Accelerator, region-dependent) —
  check current-year eligibility and windows at the Play Console → Grow →
  opportunities, or the Indie Games site. Fit: a small-team, high-depth sim.
- **ProductivITY/quality signals:** enroll in and pass **Play's technical quality
  guidelines** (low ANR/crash rates, target latest API) — editorial won't feature an
  app that trips quality thresholds.
