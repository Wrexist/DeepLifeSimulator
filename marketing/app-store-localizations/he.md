## notes

```text
- Subtitle leads with "סימולטור חיים" — the highest-volume Hebrew genre query — plus "מאפס למיליונים" (from zero to millions), a native rags-to-riches hook that also covers millionaire-intent searches; "מיליונר" is therefore left out of keywords to avoid concept duplication.
- Keywords focus on the money/strategy cluster Israelis actually type: כסף (money), השקעות (investments), עסקים (business), בורסה (stock market), נדלן (real estate, spelled without gershayim as typed in search), עושר (wealth), מיליארדר (billionaire), טייקון (tycoon).
- "משחק" is included only to combine with subtitle's "חיים" for the high-volume phrase "משחק חיים" — never as "game" alone. English "life simulator" is NOT repeated since the Latin app name already contains it, and Israelis searching in English hit the brand name directly.
- Body copy spells נדל"ן with gershayim (correct written Hebrew) while the keyword field uses נדלן (how people type in search boxes).
- Currency localized to shekels (10,000 ₪) in the compound-interest bullet; lottery reference kept (לוטו is culturally standard in Israel).
- Tone: natural, direct second person masculine (standard Israeli game-marketing register), with colloquial touches like "בלי שקל" and "פשוט רגל בסטייל".
- A/B test suggestion: subtitle variant "סימולטור חיים: כסף ובחירות" vs current; also test whether "קזינו/הימורים"-adjacent terms are worth avoiding entirely given Israeli store policy sensitivities (current set stays clean).
```
