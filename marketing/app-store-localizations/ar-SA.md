## notes

```text
- Subtitle leads with "محاكاة الحياة" (life simulation) - the highest-volume Arabic genre query - plus "اصنع ثروتك" (build your fortune), which puts the money/wealth hook directly in an indexed field. "ثروة" is therefore excluded from keywords to avoid duplication.
- Keywords deliberately include "محاكي" (simulator) alongside subtitle's "محاكاة": Gulf users type "محاكي الحياة" as often as "محاكاة الحياة", and Apple indexes them as distinct Arabic tokens. "لعبة" is included only to form the high-volume combo "لعبة الحياة" with subtitle's "الحياة" (never as "game" alone).
- Money terms cover both registers: "فلوس" (colloquial Gulf, very high volume) and "مال" (MSA), plus aspiration terms "ثري" and "مليونير" that dominate Arabic sim/tycoon search.
- English "tycoon" kept in Latin script - Gulf users genuinely search it in English; "life simulator" NOT repeated since the app name already contains it.
- Currency localized to Saudi Riyal (10,000 ريال) in the compound-growth bullet; "lottery wins" softened to "مكاسب مفاجئة" (unexpected windfalls) for Gulf cultural fit.
- Tone: MSA with light Gulf warmth (فاضٍ، فلوس) and direct second-person address, matching how top-grossing sim/tycoon games write for the Saudi storefront.
- A/B test suggestion: subtitle variant "محاكاة الحياة: من الصفر للقمة" (from zero to the top) vs the current fortune-focused line; also test فلوس-first vs استثمار-first ordering in the keyword field after 4 weeks of search-console data.
```
