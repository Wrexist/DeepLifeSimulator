# DeepLife Simulator — Consolidated Audit (2026-06-21)

Five parallel deep audits (Economy/Balance, Performance, Crash/Stability, Game
Logic, Save/State Integrity) across `contexts/game/**`, `lib/**`, `components/**`,
`services/**`, `utils/**`. Every finding below was verified against source by the
auditing pass. The codebase is genuinely hardened — most classic vectors
(week-vs-weeksLived discipline, double-spend re-entrancy, level clamping, NaN
guards, migration chain v2→20, save prune caps) are already correct. These are
the **new, real, currently-present** issues.

## P0 — Game-breaking / freeze / lost money

1. **[ECON] Real-estate sale has zero friction → deterministic money printer**
   `lib/realEstate/operations.ts:118-138` (driven by `lib/realEstate/weeklyTick.ts:55-59`)
   Sale proceeds = `currentValue − debt`, no realtor/closing fee, `capitalGain`
   computed but never taxed (only logged). Value appreciates almost every tick
   (legacy +0.1%/wk + cycle boost). Buy a leveraged property, hold, sell → net
   positive every time; with 10%-down tiers you capture 100% of appreciation on a
   10% stake. **Fix:** apply ~6% closing cost on `value` and/or capital-gains tax
   on `gain` inside `sellProperty` before returning proceeds.

2. **[LOGIC] `business_opportunity` payout never fires — `totalStages` hardcoded**
   `contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx:2180` (+ `lib/events/engine.ts:2691`)
   Chain started with `totalStages: 3` but `business_opportunity` has 4 stages, so
   the completion check (`:2154`) force-clears `activeEventChain` after stage 2 and
   the `biz_results` payout (+$6k/+$3k) never generates. Player loses their
   $1–2k investment with no return. **Fix:**
   `totalStages: eventChainDefinitions.find(c => c.chainId === event.chainId)?.stages.length ?? 3`.

3. **[PERF] Hard `await setTimeout(50)` on the critical path of every Next Week**
   `contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx:1693`
   After the main updater, `nextWeek()` sleeps a fixed 50 ms before reading
   `gameStateRef.current`; `setIsLoading(false)` only fires after that, with
   validation/save serialized behind it. Guaranteed per-tick stall — a prime
   "froze for a beat" cause. **Fix:** drop the sleep (use `await Promise.resolve()`
   if a microtask is needed, or move post-validation into a `useEffect` keyed on
   `weeksLived`).

4. **[PERF] 2–4 sequential `setGameState` commits per Next Week → multiple re-renders**
   `contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx:374` + `:1709` + `:1729` + `:1770`
   The main updater plus post-`await` validation-repair, 10-week relationship
   repair, and automation each commit separately (outside any batch). With the
   dashboard subscribing to full state (see #14–16) that's up to 4 full re-renders
   per press. **Fix:** fold post-updater mutations into the main updater / coalesce
   to one extra commit.

## P1 — Crash, strong exploit, or clearly wrong behavior

5. **[CRASH] Auto-reinvest crashes the weekly tick on a malformed holding**
   `contexts/game/actions/weekly/applyAutoReinvest.ts:95-96` and `:104-105`
   Line 66 builds `validHoldings` (filters `null`/symbol-less), but the purchase
   path reverts to raw `holdings` → `h.symbol.toUpperCase()` throws on a bad entry,
   inside the unattended weekly tick. **Fix:** use `validHoldings` for `.find`/`.map`
   and guard the callback (`h?.symbol && …`).

6. **[LOGIC] Dead player still earns income, ages, and records a full final week**
   `contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx` weekly updater (~`731` income, death
   checks at `765`/`794`/`823`/`1038`)
   All death checks run after income is credited and age/year advanced; the
   `deathTriggered` early-return (`:1743`) only skips automation + the extra save,
   not the already-committed economics. Death-ribbon + lifetime "final net worth"
   are taken on the inflated post-income state. **Fix:** when a death flag is set,
   preserve prevState money/age/year and only write death-tracking fields.

7. **[ECON] IPO double-tap double-credits cash**
   `contexts/game/actions/HustleActions.ts:571, 613-614`
   The "already public" guard reads stale outer `gameState`, and
   `updateMoney(cashRaised)` is a trailing dispatch outside the `withOverlay`
   updater. Double-tap before re-render → cash credited twice. **Fix:** fold
   cashRaised/reputation into the updater and re-check `o.ipo.status` against `prev`.

8. **[ECON] `investment_tip` event has +25% expected value**
   `lib/events/engine.ts:1509-1510` — win pays `+amount`, loss only `-amount/2`.
   Repeatable positive-EV money. **Fix:** make downside `-amount`.

9. **[ECON] Crypto limit-BUY affordability omits fees/spread → negative money**
   `contexts/game/actions/CryptoTradingActions.ts:150-156` — reserves raw notional;
   stock sibling correctly uses `(1+STOCK_FEE)`. Fill costs more than reserved
   (`lib/crypto/orderBook.ts:60,124`). **Fix:** reserve `amount * (1+fee+spread)`.

10. **[ECON] Uncapped money credits bypass `MONEY_CEILING`/finite guards**
    `BankingActions.ts:262` (redeemRewards), `:102` (withdraw), `LoanActions.ts:198`
    (loan principal) — raw `money + amount` writes skipping the mandatory
    `applyMoneyDelta` clamp; near MAX_SAFE_INTEGER → Infinity → `validateGameState`
    resets money to 0 (data loss). **Fix:** route through `applyMoneyDelta` / clamp.

11. **[ECON] Rental weekly tick credits gross rent with no upkeep offset**
    `lib/realEstate/weeklyTick.ts:43-73` — accumulates only `rentReceived`; legacy
    net (carried upkeep/tax) is zeroed. Maintenance only on manual call. **Fix:**
    net tax/maintenance into `rentalIncome`.

12. **[ECON] `maintenanceCost` 50× off vs its own doc**
    `lib/realEstate/operations.ts:324-330` — formula yields $12,500 where the
    comment says $250 (multiplier `0.05` vs intended `0.001`). Compounds #11.

13. **[ECON] `payDownCard` cash debit decoupled from actual card reduction**
    `contexts/game/actions/BankingActions.ts:221-233` — local `paid` independent of
    what `payCreditCard` applied; divergence leaks cash, `Math.max(0,…)` hides it.
    **Fix:** derive debit from the helper's actual applied delta.

14. **[CRASH] IAP purchase listener leaked + acknowledged txns never finished**
    `services/IAPService.ts:1069-1072, 1558-1562` — `destroy()` never unsets the
    listener (stacks on re-init); acknowledged branch returns without
    `finishTransactionAsync` → Android redelivery loop. **Fix:** unset listener in
    `destroy()`, gate registration, finish acknowledged transactions.

15. **[CRASH] `LifeMomentModal` has no dismiss path except a choice → soft-lock**
    `components/LifeMomentModal.tsx:76` — `<Modal>` with no `onRequestClose`, no X,
    no backdrop tap; if `pendingMoment.choices` is empty/malformed, zero controls
    render → permanent lock; Android back dead. **Fix:** add `onRequestClose` that
    clears `pendingMoment` + a fallback Dismiss when `choices.length === 0`.

16. **[LOGIC] Follow-up event chain system is dead code (queues forever)**
    `lib/events/lifeEvents.ts:199-212` — `getFollowUpEvent`/`removePendingEvent`
    never called; 8 follow-up chains never fire and `pendingChainedEvents` grows
    unbounded (save bloat). **Fix:** wire `getFollowUpEvent` into the weekly tick
    and dequeue on hit (or remove the producer).

17. **[LOGIC] Education exam/campus cadence drifts across pauses**
    `lib/education/educationSystem.ts:565-582` (consumed `applyEducationProgression.ts:107`)
    Cadence measured vs absolute `weeksLived` but `lastExamWeek` only advances on
    non-paused ticks → after a pause an exam fires immediately and stays "due".
    **Fix:** track active study weeks, or bump markers forward by paused duration.

18. **[PERF] Home screen subscribes to the WHOLE gameState**
    `app/(tabs)/home.tsx:98` (`useGame()`) — new `gameState` identity each tick →
    entire dashboard subtree re-renders every tick, defeating the selector/memo
    work on its children. **Fix:** `useGameSelector` slices + `React.memo` the cards.

19. **[PERF] IdentityCard full subscription + eager Cash-Flow breakdown loops**
    `components/IdentityCard.tsx:114` (`useGame()`); breakdown IIFEs `:816-1219`;
    memos `:156-210` keyed on `stats.money` (changes every tick). Heaviest render
    body; breakdown built even while the modal is closed. **Fix:** `useGameSelector`;
    gate breakdown on `{showCash && …}`; key net-worth memo on asset arrays.

20. **[PERF] FinanceOverview still on `useGame()` full subscription**
    `components/FinanceOverview.tsx:48` — recomputes loan/realEstate/vehicle/company
    reductions every tick. **Fix:** `useGameSelector` + `React.memo`.

21. **[SAVE] HustleApp lifetime counters never persist (silent lost-write)**
    `contexts/game/actions/HustleActions.ts:255-256` (+ `311`, `365`, `495`, `590`, `640`)
    Mutators call `ensureHustle(prev)` a 2nd time (fresh clone) to bump
    `lifetimeStats`, but `withOverlay` only keeps the overlay return — the bumped
    clone is discarded. `totalNamedHires/Fires/CampaignsRun/ScandalsSurvived/
    IPOsLaunched/AcquisitionsCompleted/peakSharePrice` stay frozen → wrong stats,
    broken achievements. **Fix:** have `withOverlay` pass its `ha` into the mutator.

## Recommended fix order (cross-domain, highest ROI first)
1. **#3** remove the 50 ms tick stall — trivial, directly addresses the freeze.
2. **#4 + #18/#19/#20** coalesce commits + migrate home/IdentityCard/FinanceOverview
   off full-state subscription — kills the multi-render dashboard jank.
3. **#5** guard auto-reinvest holdings — prevents a weekly-tick crash.
4. **#2** fix `business_opportunity` totalStages — stops silently eating investments.
5. **#1 + #11/#12** real-estate sale friction + rental upkeep — closes the biggest
   money printer.
6. **#6** short-circuit economics on death; **#21** HustleApp stat lost-write;
   **#15** LifeMomentModal soft-lock; **#7/#9/#10** money exploits.
