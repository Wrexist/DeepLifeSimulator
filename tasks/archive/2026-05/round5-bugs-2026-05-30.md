# Round 5 — Polish pass

Round 4 finished the medium-severity economy hardening. Round 5 mops up the remaining items: more Image fallbacks, a few subtle game-mechanic bugs, and the first slice of accessibility wins.

## Applied

- [components/mobile/Pulse/screens/PostDetailScreen.tsx](components/mobile/Pulse/screens/PostDetailScreen.tsx) — author avatar + post photo

### R5-B — Loan principal credit on accept
[contexts/game/actions/LoanActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/LoanActions.ts) — `acceptLoan` now ALSO credits `stats.money` with the principal, not just the bank account balance. Previously the weekly tick's `mirrorAccountsFromLegacy` overwrote `checking-default` with `stats.money`, so on the very first tick after taking a loan, the principal vanished — the player was left with the repayment obligation and no cash. Now `stats.money` is the canonical source and the bank balance mirrors it.

### R5-C — Pet sleep weekly cooldown
[contexts/game/actions/PetActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/PetActions.ts) — `petSleep` now tracks `lastSleepWeek` per pet and rate-limits to once per week. Previously the player could spam Sleep before any competition to keep the pet at 100/100, trivializing competition value and the toy/diet/medicine economy.

Added `lastSleepWeek?: number` to `Pet` in [contexts/game/types.ts](contexts/game/types.ts).

### R5-D — Insurance pro-rata refund
[contexts/game/actions/VehicleActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/VehicleActions.ts) `cancelInsurance` now returns a pro-rata refund (weeks remaining × monthly cost / 4) minus a $25 admin fee. Previously the "no refund" behavior turned insurance into a single-claim rental exploit — buy 6 months, force/wait for an accident, take the repair discount, cancel for free. Pro-rata refund removes the cancel-after-claim arbitrage without punishing legitimate cancels.

### R5-E — `joinMiningPool` cooldown
[contexts/game/actions/MiningActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/MiningActions.ts) — 1-week cooldown between pool changes. Previously a player could hop between pools every action chasing the best `bonusMultiplier - fee` for the current crypto. Initial join (no `activePool` yet) is exempt so players can pick their first pool freely.

### R5-F — Credit card rewards redemption
[lib/banking/operations.ts](lib/banking/operations.ts) — new `redeemCardRewards(banking, cardId)` helper returns the dollar amount that should be deposited into `stats.money`, capped at $10,000 per call so a corrupt card with Infinity pending rewards can't crash the cash slice. Wired into a new `redeemRewards` action in [contexts/game/actions/BankingActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/BankingActions.ts) that atomically updates both `banking` and `stats.money`.

Closes the round-2 finding: pending rewards used to accumulate on every charge with no way to redeem them.

### R5-G — Accessibility props on sub-app launchers
- [app/(tabs)/computer.tsx](app/(tabs)/computer.tsx) — category tab buttons got `accessibilityRole="tab"` + `accessibilityState.selected`; each app card got `accessibilityRole="button"` + `accessibilityLabel="Open {appName}"` + `accessibilityHint`.
- [app/(tabs)/mobile.tsx](app/(tabs)/mobile.tsx) — same pattern on the mobile app cards.

VoiceOver/TalkBack now correctly announces each launcher icon as a button with a meaningful name. The deeper sub-apps still need their own accessibility passes, but these are the entry points users hit most.
