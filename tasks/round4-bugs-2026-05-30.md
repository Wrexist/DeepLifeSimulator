Round 3 finished the structural deferred items. Round 4 cleans up the medium-severity economy exploits from round 2's audit and the long tail of TextInput/Image hygiene.

### R4-B — `ImageWithFallback` helper
New [components/ui/ImageWithFallback.tsx](components/ui/ImageWithFallback.tsx) — drop-in replacement that gracefully degrades to an initial-letter placeholder on URI load failure. Reset-on-uri-change so a failed URI doesn't stick. Wired into [PostCard](components/mobile/Pulse/components/PostCard.tsx) (avatar + post photo — rendered N times per feed) and [CommentItem](components/mobile/Pulse/components/CommentItem.tsx) (avatar). Remaining Pulse/Spark screens can adopt the same pattern incrementally.

### R4-C — Pet inventory race
[contexts/game/actions/PetActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/PetActions.ts) `feedPet` now does the inventory decrement and pet stat update in a single atomic `setGameState`. Two rapid feeds with inventory=1 used to both pass the outer check, both fire setState, and the second decrement landed at -1. Now the inside-updater rechecks `prev.petFood[foodId] > 0` and returns `prev` if zero.

### R4-D — Wedding + propose double-charge
[contexts/game/actions/DatingActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/DatingActions.ts) `proposeMarriage` (accepted + rejected branches) and `executeWedding` now re-check affordability AND the relationship state inside the `setGameState(prev =>)` updater. A same-batch double-tap on Propose used to deduct the ring price twice for one engagement; on Wedding it could deduct the remaining 75% twice. Both paths now bail out if the operation already happened in this batch.

### R4-E — Mining upgrade race recheck
[contexts/game/actions/MiningActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/MiningActions.ts) `buyMinerUpgrade` recomputes the level, cost, and affordability from `prev` inside the updater. Previously a same-batch double-tap from level 4 could buy levels 5 AND 6 simultaneously, double-debiting money in the process.

### R4-F — Credit card NaN limit + payment history
[lib/banking/operations.ts](lib/banking/operations.ts):
- `chargeCreditCard` now rejects when `creditLimit` is NaN/Infinity/non-positive. The previous `balance + amt > creditLimit` evaluated `NaN > NaN === false`, so a corrupt card would let the player charge anything.
- `payCreditCard` now writes `lastPaymentWeek` + increments `onTimePayments`. Previously paying off cards never improved the credit score (only missed payments could decrease it — a one-sided ratchet).

### R4-G — Disease worsening compound cap
[contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx](contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx) — chronic untreated disease effects compound at 10% per week. Previously this was uncapped: a 100-week untreated disease ended up multiplying effects by ~13,780×, eventually producing Infinity and NaN that poisoned the entire stats slice. Now capped at 3× the original effect (or -100 floor for stat penalties), with `baseEffects` stored separately so a cure-then-reinfect resets the compounding.

### R4-H — `dailySummary.statsChange` clamp
[contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx](contexts/game/GameActionsContext.tsx) — clamps each statsChange accumulator to ±1000 inside `updateStats`. Stats themselves are already clamped to [0,100], so a delta beyond ±1000 represents many weeks without summary dismissal — clamping keeps the summary display sane and the save bytecount bounded.

### R4-I — Staking double-claim fix
[contexts/game/actions/MiningActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/MiningActions.ts) `claimStakingRewards` now does the entire reward calculation INSIDE the `setGameState(prev =>)` updater, using `prev.warehouse.stakingPositions` as the source of truth. Previously the calculation ran against the outer snapshot, so two rapid taps both saw the same available rewards and both credited them — particularly bad at the maturity boundary where principal was also returned twice.

### R4-J — `tickBillPay` negative-amount guard
[lib/banking/operations.ts](lib/banking/operations.ts) — skips bill-pay rules whose amount is non-finite/≤0 instead of treating them as missed payments. Previously `withdrawFromAccount`'s `safe()` would coerce a bad amount to 0, the rule would be marked missed, and a $35 late fee got debited for a fictional debt.

### R4-K — `applyForJob` inside-updater pending recheck
[contexts/game/actions/JobActions.ts](contexts/game/actions/JobActions.ts) — re-checks `pendingApplication` and `currentJob` inside the updater. Previously a same-batch double-tap on two different careers could both mark themselves accepted, corrupting `careerHistory` and potentially the "maxed careers" prestige bonus.
