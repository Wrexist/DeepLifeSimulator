Privacy Policy — DeepLife Simulator

Last updated: 23 July 2026

DeepLife Simulator ("the app," "we," "us") is a single-player life-simulation game. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data we collect

DataWhyVia
Advertising ID & device informationTo show and measure adsGoogle AdMob
App-usage events & device identifiersAnalytics to improve the gameFirebase Analytics
Crash & diagnostic dataTo fix crashes and improve stabilitySentry
Purchase historyTo deliver in-app purchases you makeGoogle Play / App Store billing

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages. The app has no account login; cloud saves are tied to an app-generated identifier, not to your personal identity.

How data is shared

Advertising and device identifiers are shared with Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. Analytics data is processed by Google Firebase, and crash/diagnostic data by Sentry, acting as our processors. We do not sell your data. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play and the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details.

Ads & tracking

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. On iOS, we ask for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads; you can decline. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children and contains mature simulated themes (finance, debt, an "underground economy"). It is intended for a teen/adult audience per its store age rating.

Data retention & deletion

Cloud-save data is retained while you use the app. To request deletion of data associated with your app identifier, contact us at the email below and we will action it within a reasonable period. Uninstalling the app removes local data from your device.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking (iOS ATT prompt) or reset your advertising ID (device settings).
  • Purchase DeepLife+ or Remove Ads to stop seeing ads.
  • Request data deletion via the contact email.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
